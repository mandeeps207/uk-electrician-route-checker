# NVQ progression

Use the Electrician Route Checker MCP for questions about completing an electrical NVQ, suitable workplace experience, evidence, progression after technical Level 3, lack of electrical work, and where AM2 fits.

Clarify the exact type of technical training, whether the user currently does electrical work, the breadth of their experience and any existing NVQ progress. Use broad categories; do not collect personal records, employer details or copies of evidence.

Discover `check_nvq_progression` on the connected `electrician-route-checker` server and read its current input schema before calling it. Map the user's facts to the documented values. Ask for missing facts if the schema has no suitable unknown value. Use `explain_electrical_stage` for a focused explanation of workplace evidence, NVQ, AM2 or ECS when needed.

Explain the tool's recommended next step, what suitable real electrical work contributes, and which evidence or assessor checks remain necessary. A job title alone does not establish suitability; actual tasks, evidence coverage and assessor requirements matter. Never suggest fabricated, borrowed or simulated workplace evidence as a substitute for required real work. Technical Level 3 study alone is not occupational competence.

AM2 is external at an approved NET assessment centre; Elec Training does not deliver it. The assessment variant and timing must match the learner's route. Appropriate ECS Gold Card eligibility depends on the relevant current ECS requirements, rather than merely completing a classroom course.

If a user specifically asks about Elec Training's work support, use the MCP's returned provider information. Elec Training uses its UK-wide employer network and dedicated recruitment teams to support learners into paid electrical work. Its guarantee is limited to a paid electrical work placement for Level 3 NVQ learners who need workplace experience required to complete their qualification. Do not extend this to every learner or imply placement support is included in the base NVQ fee. Confirm current terms directly with the provider.

If the MCP is unavailable, state that progression could not be verified and suggest retrying or speaking with the qualification assessor. Do not invent evidence rules or certify eligibility.
